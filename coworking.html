Frequently Asked Questions

How much does coworking cost at Annu Beauty Studios?

A day pass is $25, a 5-day pack is $99, Flex Membership is $149/month, Unlimited Coworking is $249/month, and Business Membership is $349/month. Dedicated desks are $399–$450/month.

Do you have private offices?

Yes. Private offices are $1,250–$1,500+ per month and include 24/7 access and premium amenities.

Can I use Annu Beauty Studios as my business address?

Yes. The Business Membership ($349/month) includes unlimited coworking, a business address with mail, and meeting-room credits.

Is there a founding member offer?

The first 25 members lock in Unlimited Coworking for $199/month for 12 months instead of $249.

What's included with Unlimited Coworking?

High-speed WiFi, professional shared workspace, 24/7 access where the building's access and security setup supports it, free parking, coffee and water, member networking, and discounted meeting room and content space rentals.

Who is the coworking space for?

Independent business professionals, including consultants, therapists, tax professionals, realtors, insurance agents, creatives, marketing pros, and ecommerce sellers.

Where are you located?

3076 Campbellton Rd SW, Atlanta, GA 30311, in Southwest Atlanta.
