Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a salon suite cost at Annu Beauty Studios?

Suites are $175–$400 per week or $600–$1,400 per month, depending on the suite. Founding tenants lock in discounted, below-market rates.

Do I have to sign a long-term lease?

No. Suites are leased weekly or monthly with no long-term lock-in.

What's included with a salon suite?

High-speed WiFi, a smart door lock, individual climate control, professional lighting and mirrors, a shared washer/dryer and product storage, a content creation corner with ring lights and a backdrop, and a Complimentary Business Growth Consultation.

I'm not ready for a full suite. Do you have other options?

Yes. Daily booth rental is $45 per day, a simple way to start before moving into your own suite.

Who are the suites for?

Licensed beauty and wellness professionals, including hair stylists, locticians and natural hair specialists, estheticians, lash artists, nail technicians, massage therapists, and permanent makeup artists.

Where are you located?

3076 Campbellton Rd SW, Atlanta, GA 30311, on Campbellton Road in Southwest Atlanta.

How do I tour a suite?

Request a tour online or call (470) 502-5624, and we'll follow up with availability and tour times.
